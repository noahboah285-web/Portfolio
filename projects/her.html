← Back

For Her, the goal was to translate the feeling of looking back on a love that still lingers. Inspired by the artist's memories of falling deeply in love, the film explores the space where affection and nostalgia quietly intersect, allowing emotion to unfold without relying on a literal narrative.

The visuals are built around soft natural light, intimate compositions, and an unhurried rhythm that mirrors the restraint of the piano. Fleeting moments, lingering glances, and empty spaces become as important as the subjects themselves, creating the feeling of remembering someone through fragments rather than complete memories. The result is a film that invites viewers to revisit their own experiences of love, longing, and the people who continue to shape us long after they've gone.
