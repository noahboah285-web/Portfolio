Between Brothers was conceived as a quiet tribute to the artist's late uncle, a film shaped by the experience of grief, remembrance, and the conversations that can no longer take place. Rather than portraying loss directly, the visual narrative focuses on the emotional space it leaves behind, finding meaning in silence, absence, and the memories that continue to endure.

With restrained cinematography and a contemplative pace, the film allows each frame to breathe, reflecting the intimacy of the song's dedication. Natural landscapes, solitary moments, and subtle visual motifs evoke the bond between two brothers while hinting at the legacy they leave for those who remain. As the first chapter in a larger body of work, paired with a future piece dedicated to the artist's father, the film explores how love and family continue to guide us, even after someone is gone.
