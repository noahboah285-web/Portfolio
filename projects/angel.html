For Angel, I set out to create a film that felt less like a traditional music video and more like a sequence of scenes pulled from a feature film. The creative direction centered on quiet, contemplative moments that invite viewers to look beyond the performance and reflect on the lives unfolding within the frame.

Using restrained camera movement, atmospheric lighting, and a deliberate pace, the visuals prioritize emotion over spectacle. Each shot is designed to linger, allowing space for interpretation and encouraging the audience to question themes of purpose, memory, and the paths we choose. Rather than illustrating the lyrics directly, the film creates an experience that leaves a lasting impression that continues to resonate after the final frame.
